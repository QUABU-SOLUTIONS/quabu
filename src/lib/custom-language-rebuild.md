For many teams, working every day in their native or local language is not just a matter of comfort: it improves tool adoption, reduces misunderstandings in workflows and makes the user experience far more natural.

However, those of us who work in the Atlassian ecosystem know that native language support in Jira Cloud has its limits. Co-official, regional or minority languages are left out of the standard catalog, forcing users to work in English or Spanish by default.

That is why **Custom Language for Jira Cloud** was born, and that is why today we are taking a giant step to bring it to the next level.

## Why We Decided to Start from Scratch

When we launched the first version of our app, the goal was clear: solve a real need for language customization. However, as the project grew and we received *feedback* from the community, we realized that to offer a truly smooth, fast and scalable experience, we needed to rethink the foundations.

At **Quabu Solutions** we have made a bold engineering decision: **completely refactor the app from scratch with an entirely new architecture**.

### What Changes with This New Architecture?

- **Instant language switching from the browser:** Users will be able to switch between languages directly from our browser extension, with no complex configuration or friction in their day-to-day work.
- **Greater performance and stability:** The new technical structure loads dictionaries much more lightly and adapts more resiliently to the constant updates of the Jira Cloud interface.
- **Scalability for new dictionaries:** The new engine makes it much easier to create, maintain and add new language packs in record time.

## Catalan, Basque… and Much More on the Way!

In this new stage we have focused on bringing Jira closer to language communities that had long been asking for their place.

In the internal tests we are already running, the results with the **Catalan** and **Basque** dictionaries are excellent. But this is just the beginning: on our immediate roadmap we are already preparing the arrival of **Galician**, **Aranese** and even **Sicilian**.

## Your Language Can Be in Jira Too

We want this new version of **Custom Language for Jira Cloud** to grow alongside the users who rely on it. Our new dictionary system is designed to be open and adaptable.

**Missing your favorite language in Jira?** We would love to hear from you. If your company or community needs to work in a language that is not yet supported, get in touch with us and we will work together to add its dictionary to upcoming updates.
