How many hours do you waste each week in meetings that could be resolved in minutes? **Loom**, the recording platform acquired by **Atlassian**, was created precisely to replace unnecessary video calls with clear, asynchronous video messages.

Instead of blocking out two hours in your calendar to explain something, you can record your screen in a moment and spend the rest of your time doing real work and creating value for the business. What's more, thanks to its **AI-powered features**, Loom automatically generates summaries, chapters and tasks from your recordings.
