We are excited to announce that we have submitted our new application, **Visual Organizer for Jira Cloud**, to the **Atlassian Hackathon 2026**.

This application was born out of a very real need we see every day in organizations: **easing the impact of change after migrating from Jira Server or Jira Data Center to Jira Cloud**.

## A Frictionless Landing in Jira Cloud

When a company completes its migration to the cloud, the technical challenge ends, but the challenge of **user adoption** begins. People who have spent years working with Jira Server or Data Center often feel disoriented by Jira Cloud's interface and navigation changes, slowing their initial productivity and creating resistance to change.

With **Visual Organizer for Jira Cloud**, we have built a bridge between both worlds: **we keep the toolbar in the header with the same classic Data Center layout**. This allows teams to keep working from day one in a familiar environment, reducing the learning curve and gradually adapting to Jira Cloud's new features at their own pace.

## How Does It Improve the Everyday Experience?

In addition to preserving the navigation structure in the header, the app adds visual tools designed to make organizing everyday work much faster:

- **Data Center-style Header Toolbar:** Keeps the familiar layout in the header so you can quickly access your key items without having to retrain the entire team's muscle memory.
- **Custom labels and colors:** Identify projects, boards and dashboards at a glance with color codes and tailored visual labels.
- **Smart Categories & Global Scopes:** Organize resources neatly, both individually and in a shared structure for the entire organization.
- **Direct access to favorites:** Keep your most frequently used boards, projects and dashboards within easy reach at the top.

## Innovating to Make the Transition to the Cloud Easier

Taking part in the Atlassian Hackathon 2026 has been the ideal setting to bring this idea to life and focus on the most important part of any migration: **the people who use the tool every day**.
