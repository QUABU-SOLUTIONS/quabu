Until now, Atlassian's **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** let you connect AI assistants and chat platforms to the standard Jira and Confluence environment to manage generic work items or documentation. However, for support and operations (ITSM) teams, a key piece was missing: service-specific context.

With the new **Atlassian MCP** update, the protocol expands its reach by adding native tools designed exclusively for **Jira Service Management (JSM)**.

In service management, work no longer happens only inside the ITSM console; it starts in Slack, Microsoft Teams or in assistants like Claude. Now, MCP-compatible agents and clients can interact directly with real support and operations workflows without forcing teams to jump between tabs, securely accessing:

- **End-to-end request management:** Create, triage and resolve support tickets directly from the conversation.
- **Real-time alert control:** Acknowledge, close and escalate critical incidents before they impact the service or breach an SLA.
- **On-call schedules:** Check on-call calendars and team availability instantly.
- **Asset search (Assets/CMDB):** Locate resources, configurations and infrastructure linked to an incident.
- **Knowledge base:** Draft new help articles from recent resolutions.

Whereas before it was only possible to work with conventional Jira issues, this evolution turns Atlassian MCP into a true operational bridge for JSM, bringing critical context exactly where your team works.
